🌊 What Is This?

Northern Beaches Surf Check is an automated surf report for 8 Sydney Northern Beaches. It runs every morning, fetches live marine and weather data, and produces a detailed report with wave heights, surf quality ratings, board recommendations, and wetsuit advice.

Each report covers 4 timeframes per day (morning, midday, afternoon, evening) across 3 days, so you can plan your session.

⭐ How to Read the Ratings

Each beach gets a color-coded quality badge so you can scan the report quickly:

The rating balances wave height and wave quality. A small clean day can rate well, and a big messy day won't rate well just because it's big.

🏄 What Affects the Rating

Seven factors are combined to produce the final rating:

  • 🌊 Wave height — Bigger is better, up to a point. Beyond overhead, quality matters more.
  • 💨 Wind — Offshore wind holds the wave face up and makes it steeper. Onshore wind flattens it. The report shows both the wind direction relative to the beach and whether it's hitting the wave face or going off the back.
  • 🌊 Swell period — Long-period groundswell (12s+) produces clean, powerful waves. Short-period windswell (< 8s) is weaker and less organised.
  • 🌊 Tide — Mid-tide on a rising trend is generally best. Very low or very high tides can hurt quality.
  • 🏞 Beach shape — Narrow beaches (like Freshwater) close out on big swells but produce punchy peaks on small days. Wide beaches handle size better.
  • 🌊 How the wave breaks — Steep beaches produce punchier waves. Flat beaches produce mushier waves that need more tide to work.
  • 🌊 Wind-sea chop — Local wind chop on top of the swell makes conditions bumpy and messy.

🌊 Wave Heights

Heights are reported as face height — the vertical trough-to-crest of the breaking wave, in Australian convention. This is what you actually see in the water, not the open-ocean significant wave height.

Face height depends on the swell period (longer period = bigger face relative to offshore height) and wind (offshore wind holding the face up makes it taller).

Heights are shown as a range (e.g. 3-5 ft) when the swell spectrum is broad, or a single value when it's clean and consistent.

🤖 Surf Notes

The daily assessment, per-beach notes, and 7-day outlook are generated by an AI model. It receives all the computed data — wave heights, wind, tide, beach characteristics — and synthesises it into plain-English surf commentary.

The notes are qualitative and focus on what the conditions mean for your session, not raw numbers.

📡 Data Sources

  • Today’s wave data — Botany Offshore buoy (real-time observations from Port Authority NSW). Scraped every 3 hours at 05:45, 08:00, 11:00, 14:00.
  • Tomorrow+ wave data — Open-Meteo Marine API (ECMWF WAM spectral wave model), cached 6 hours.
  • Wind & weather — Open-Meteo Weather API
  • Water temperature — IMOS satellite SST (RAMSSA L4 analysis), with fallback to monthly averages if satellite data is unavailable
  • Tide — Harmonic tide model calibrated for Fort Denison

Forecast and buoy observations are logged side-by-side for ongoing accuracy monitoring. See Model page for full technical details.
